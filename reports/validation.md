# Validation (model selection)

Validation window: 2026-07-12 to 2026-07-19 (last 7 training days, 1204 emails,
907 legit by observed label, 22 hard positives). Everything was fitted on emails before
2026-07-12 only. Scores use the observed (noisy) labels. Generated by `make validate`.

## LightGBM settings (fixed: learning_rate=0.05, num_leaves=15, subsample=0.8, subsample_freq=1, colsample_bytree=0.8)

| n_estimators | num_leaves | min_child_samples | reg_lambda | val PR-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 15 | 20 | 0.0 | 0.9546 |
| 150 | 15 | 20 | 0.0 | 0.9604 |
| 300 | 7 | 40 | 0.0 | 0.9577 |
| 300 | 15 | 40 | 5.0 | 0.9540 |
| 150 | 15 | 40 | 5.0 | 0.9520 |

Chosen: n_estimators=150, num_leaves=15, min_child_samples=20, reg_lambda=0.0 (best validation PR-AUC).
**Config matches.**

## Gray-zone lower bound (upper bound 0.8)

Rule: the most hard positives caught while flagging at most 1% of legit mail
(≤9.1 emails here). "Flagged" = any action but DELIVER.

|  | sent to Tier 2 | recall | hard-positive recall | legit flagged | legit quarantined / spam |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 0.7% | 94.3% | 82% | 10 | 6 |
| 0.2 | 0.7% | 94.3% | 82% | 11 | 6 |
| 0.1 | 1.7% | 94.9% | 91% | 18 | 7 |
| 0.05 | 3.1% | 94.9% | 91% | 31 | 7 |

Chosen: **0.3**. No bound meets the budget, so the most conservative candidate is used (it flags the fewest legit emails). **Config matches.**
