# Multi-seed evaluation

Seeds 42, 43, 44, 45, 46: each seed regenerates the synthetic data and retrains every model, then evaluates on its own
held-out last 12 days. Values are mean ± standard deviation over 5 runs.
Hyperparameters, the gray zone and rule severities were chosen on seed 42's validation window and
kept fixed. Generated by `make multiseed`.
